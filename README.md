Have you ever wondered what it's like to be a hacker? Would you like to rob the Louvre? Our game will let you do exactly that!
Hackatouille is a point-and-click puzzle solving and platforming game where you need to aid your fellow robbers to the treasure hidden in The Louvre, exploit the vulnerabilities of outdated systems and weak passwords in order to prevail. Unless you run out of time and get caught.

Controls:
Use your mouse to interact with objects.

During platforming mini-game:
A / D to move
Space to jump
